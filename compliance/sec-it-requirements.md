# SEC IT Requirements Review

## Overview

This document provides a high-level review of the information technology requirements defined in SEC Notification No. SorThor 38/2565.

The regulation focuses on establishing effective IT Governance, Information Security, Risk Management, and IT Audit practices to support secure and resilient business operations. 

---

## Key Compliance Areas

### IT Governance

Key requirements include:

- IT Governance Framework
- IT Risk Management
- Technology Policies and Procedures
- Management Oversight and Accountability

Organizations should establish governance structures to ensure that information technology supports business objectives while maintaining appropriate risk controls. 

---

### IT Security

Core security principles include:

- Information Security Controls
- Confidentiality
- Integrity
- Availability

Security measures should be implemented to protect information assets, systems, and services from unauthorized access, modification, disclosure, or disruption. 

---

### IT Audit

Recommended control areas include:

- IT Audit Process
- Compliance Validation
- Security Assessment
- Control Effectiveness Review

Regular assessments help ensure compliance with regulatory requirements and identify opportunities for continuous improvement. 

---

## Risk Management Perspective

The notification emphasizes the importance of identifying, assessing, and managing information technology risks that may impact:

- Business Operations
- Information Security
- Data Protection
- Market Confidence

Organizations should maintain an appropriate risk assessment process and continuously monitor technology-related threats and vulnerabilities. 

---

## Manager Perspective

As an Infrastructure and Security Manager, the key focus areas include:

- Security Governance
- Regulatory Compliance
- Risk Management
- Operational Resilience
- Incident Response Readiness
- Business Continuity Planning

These requirements provide a framework for aligning technology operations with regulatory expectations and industry best practices.

---

## Key Takeaways

1. Establish effective IT Governance.
2. Maintain strong Information Security controls.
3. Perform regular IT Risk Assessments.
4. Conduct periodic IT Audits.
5. Ensure regulatory compliance and operational resilience.
6. Continuously improve cybersecurity capabilities.
